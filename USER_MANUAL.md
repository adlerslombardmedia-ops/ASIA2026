# KEFF Championship Milano 2026
## Website User Manual — Teams & Players

---

## Getting Started

Open the website on your phone or computer. No login or account is needed to view anything. The site works best on mobile.

At the bottom of the screen you will see four tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
|-----|-------------|
| **Home** | Fixtures, standings, and the knockout bracket |
| **Stats** | Top scorers, top assists, and team statistics |
| **Info** | Official announcements from the tournament director |
| **Admin** | For tournament staff only |

---

## Home Tab

The Home tab has three sections you can switch between using the buttons at the top: **Groups**, **Fixtures**, and **Knockouts**.

### Groups

Shows the current standings for all four groups (A, B, C, D).

Each row shows:
- **P** — matches played
- **W / D / L** — wins, draws, losses
- **GF / GA** — goals scored / goals conceded
- **GD** — goal difference
- **Pts** — total points

The top 2 teams from each group advance to the Quarter-Finals.

### Fixtures

Shows all group stage matches in order.

- Matches that have been played show the final score.
- Upcoming matches show the scheduled time and ground number.
- A match currently being played shows a green **LIVE NOW** indicator with the live score.

Tap any match to open the full match detail page (lineups, events, stats).

### Knockouts

Shows the bracket for the knockout stage: Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals, 3rd Place Play-off, and the Final.

- Each card shows the two teams, the score (or VS if not yet played), time, and ground.
- The **Final** is highlighted in gold.
- The **3rd Place Play-off** is highlighted in bronze.
- Tap any match to see full details.

---

## Viewing a Match

Tap any match card from the fixtures or knockouts to open the match page.

You will see:

- **Score** — final score or live score
- **Lineups** — the 7 starting players for each team, shown on a pitch diagram
- **Match Events** — goals and assists with the player name and minute
- **Shot Map** — a visual map of all shots taken during the match
- **Possession** — percentage of ball possession for each team

---

## Viewing a Team

Tap any team logo or team name anywhere on the site to open the team profile.

You will see:

- **Team logo and name**
- **Group** the team is in
- **Quick stats** — matches played, goals scored, points
- **All Fixtures** — every match the team plays, with result, opponent, time, and ground. Results are colour-coded:
  - Green border = Win
  - Red border = Loss
  - Grey border = Draw
  - Green flashing = Live match
- **Squad list** — all registered players with jersey number and position
- **Top performers** — goals and assists broken down by player
- **Shot map** — all shots the team has taken across the tournament

---

## Stats Tab

Shows tournament-wide statistics.

- **Top Scorers** — ranked list of players with the most goals
- **Top Assists** — ranked list of players with the most assists
- **Team Stats** — goals, shots, and possession averages by team

---

## Info Tab

This is the official notice board from the tournament director.

All important announcements, schedule changes, and updates will be posted here. Check this tab regularly during the tournament day.

- Messages are shown in order, newest first.
- Team mentions (shown as gold chips) can be tapped to go directly to that team's page.

---

## Day / Night Mode

Tap the **Day / Night** button in the top-right corner of the screen to switch between light and dark display. The app remembers your preference.

---

## Tips

- The website updates **in real time** — you do not need to refresh the page to see new scores or announcements.
- If a match is live, go to **Home → Fixtures** or **Home → Knockouts** and look for the green **LIVE NOW** badge.
- Tap your own team anywhere on the site to see all your upcoming and completed matches in one place.
